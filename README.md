# Airbnb-Global-Performance

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 279K+ Airbnb listings, 182K hosts and 5.37M reviews across 10 global cities to uncover market concentration, pricing, ratings and seasonal demand.

**Tools:** Power BI · Power Query · DAX · Excel

![Dashboard Overview](images/dashboard-page-1.png)

## Dataset Overview

| Listings | Hosts | Reviews | Cities | Property Types |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 279,712 | 182,024 | 5.37M | 10 | 144 |

**Cities:** Paris, New York, Sydney, Rome, Rio de Janeiro, Istanbul, Mexico City, Bangkok, Cape Town, Hong Kong

## Key Insights

- **Market concentration:** Paris, New York and Sydney account for 48.4% of listings and ~59% of reviews (Pareto analysis).
- **Listing lifecycle (2008-2020):** new listings peaked in 2015, slowed in 2016-17 amid tighter local regulations, and were disrupted by COVID-19.
- **Pricing:** average price is highest for hotel rooms ($800) and lowest for private rooms ($462).
- **Ratings:** Mexico City and Rio rate highest, Hong Kong and Istanbul lowest. Cleanliness and value score lowest overall.
- **Review behavior:** 86.5% of reviewers left only one review.
- **Seasonality:** Paris and Rome peak from April to August, New York in November and December.
- **Trust:** ~67% of hosts are identity-verified.
